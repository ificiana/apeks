Runs from 2026-11-11 on the 80-core server: `uv run apeks-run experiments/*_sweep.toml --snapshot-every 10 --logs logs/r7`. The sweep took 1 322 s of wall time. The setup is the same as report 6 (demand 1.5×, 6000 ticks, the same events, `claude-haiku-5-5` for the LLM arms), with seeds 0–49 instead of 0–10. Each run generates 456 packages on average (406–497). All 200 runs finished, with 0 LLM failures and 0 skipped calls.

Seeds 0–10 repeat report 6's seeds, but the LLM arms vary from run to run, so the two reports are not merged.

## Findings

1. **LLM routing's lead holds at 50 seeds.** Both LLM-routing arms deliver 388–392 of 456 packages against classical's 178, at 38% of its latency and 44% of its energy per delivery, in 50/50 seeds. The effect sizes (d_z 4.6–5.9) match report 6.
2. **Empty distance is now significant.** With 50 seeds the 2.3–2.8k excess of LLM routing passes Holm, which it did not at 11 seeds. LLM routing sails emptier but still delivers more.
3. **Total energy falls slightly under LLM routing.** It is 3–4k lower (36/50 seeds), significant after Holm for the combined arm only.
4. **LLM formation costs safety.** It adds 81 near misses over classical (5/50 seeds better), which report 6 could not resolve. 32 of its 50 runs exceed 200 near misses; classical's mean is 148.
5. **The combined arm does not beat LLM routing alone.** Report 6's edge of 8.5 deliveries shrinks to 4.8 and is not significant; the combined arm keeps a near-miss excess of 28.
