# Report 3: seed sweep on the realistic information model, 4 arms × 11 seeds

Runs from 2026-11-11: `uv run apeks-run experiments/*_sweep.toml --snapshot-every 10 --logs logs/r3`. Each arm ran seeds 0–10 for 6000 ticks with the same scripted events as reports 1 and 2 (storm at t=1500–2500, strait blocked at t=3000–4000). LLM arms use `claude-haiku-5-5`. All 44 runs finished, with 0 LLM failures and 0 skipped calls.

What differs from report 2 is what agents know:
- Port stations repeat only the hazards they have heard. Convoys find closures at their own node and broadcast them, and messages are relayed for up to 3 hops.
- Lane reports carry 10% noise.
- HQ (the LLM) sees only reports uplinked by ports after 30 ticks, plus a delayed, error-prone news feed with false alarms (default `[news]` settings).
- Policies are versioned and spread from ports by radio.

Every random stream now comes from the seed by hashing. A seed therefore fixes a different scenario than in report 2, so the numbers are not comparable run for run.

## Inference

The method is the same as in report 2:
- Arms are paired by seed.
- The main test is the Wilcoxon signed-rank test, with a paired t-test as a cross-check.
- Confidence intervals are 95% bootstrap intervals (10 000 resamples).
- The effect size is d_z (mean difference over the sd of the differences).
- A Holm correction runs across the 18 comparisons against classical; ✓ means significant at 0.05 after it.

- **Radio:** about 97–100k messages sent per run, 5.0% of receptions lost and no overflow. That is the same load as before; relaying adds well under 0.1%. Each LLM module adds about 1 500 policy broadcasts per run.
- **Blocked-lane discovery now happens.** Convoys find the strait blocked 1.3–2.8 times per run, against 0 in reports 1 and 2.
- **Policy spread:** across the LLM arms, 325 policy versions were decided. 317 reached the whole fleet, at a median of **40 ticks** after the decision (30 of uplink plus the wait for the next port notice). The remaining 8 were overtaken by a newer version first.
- **LLM use per module per run:** about 7.4 calls, 64–65k input tokens and 6–7k output tokens, $0.011 as reported by `claude -p`. Calls rose from 5 per module in report 1 because news items, false alarms and uplinked fleet reports all trigger requests.

## Findings

1. **LLM routing stays a large, reliable win without privileged information.**
   - It beats classical on deliveries in all 11 seeds, by 24 on average (+141%), and cuts trip time by 53%.
   - Energy per delivery falls by 60%.
   - The gain is larger than in report 2's +17. Classical lost the most from realism (16.8 deliveries, against 18.6), because ACO's pheromone now comes from noisy reports.

2. **LLM formation no longer costs deliveries, but it is unsafe.**
   - Its deliveries equal classical's (−0.4, p=0.69), against −8 in report 2.
   - It still uses less total energy (−8%).
   - Near misses almost triple (404 against 138, worse in all 11 seeds). This reverses report 2, where it cut near misses.
   - The likely cause is per-convoy policies: neighbouring convoys can run different formation versions for about 40 ticks after each change. That needs checking in the traces.

3. **The combined arm matches LLM routing on deliveries and efficiency.** The formation module's delivery cost is gone, and the effects stay additive. However, the combined arm inherits formation's safety problem, with near misses up 53% on classical (p=0.032, not significant after Holm).

4. **Beliefs are often wrong, and convoys more so than HQ.**
   - Convoys spend thousands of convoy-ticks per run believing stale closures or missing real ones.
   - HQ, fed by every port, is wrong for far fewer ticks.
   - HQ in the single-module LLM arms is wrong for far more ticks than in classical (about 1 250 against 190). News cannot change HQ's hazard book, so the gap must come from which closures and reopenings convoys witness and uplink. This is unexplained and worth investigating.

5. **Congestion is still too noisy to call.** No arm differs from classical significantly.

## Limits

- 11 seeds, one map, one model. The Wilcoxon floor of p=0.001 is reached by every 11/11 result.
- Default news settings only. The effects of news errors, false alarms and misses have not been varied.
- An LLM run is not reproducible from its seed.
